# Wrong Wi-Fi

The tablet is connected to a Wi-Fi network, but not to the one the device is on. This often happens when the tablet joins a neighbour's or a phone's hotspot by itself.

## What to do

1. Open the Wi-Fi settings and choose the plant's network (the name is usually on the Wi-Fi box or the installation sheet).
2. In the list of saved networks, tap other networks and choose **Forget**, so the tablet doesn't switch to them again.
3. Wait a minute: the values come back by themselves.

## For the installer

- If the device got a new address from the router, the app searches for it by serial number. You can also edit the device and use **Search network**.
- A fixed address (DHCP reservation in the router) avoids this.
