# No Wi-Fi

The tablet isn't connected to a Wi-Fi network, so it can't read the inverter. The inverter itself keeps working.

## What to do

1. Swipe down from the top of the screen and check that **Wi-Fi is on**.
2. Open the Wi-Fi settings and tap the plant's network. Its name and password are usually on a sticker on the Wi-Fi box (gateway or router) or on the installation sheet.
3. If the network isn't in the list: check that the Wi-Fi box has power (its lights are on). After a power cut it needs **2 to 3 minutes** to start.
4. If Android says "This network has no internet access", choose **Stay connected** and **Don't ask again**. This display doesn't need internet.

## Still not working?

- Unplug the Wi-Fi box for 10 seconds and plug it in again, then wait 3 minutes.
- Restart the tablet: hold the power button and choose Restart.
- Call your installer.
