# No mains power

The device reports that the electricity grid is down.

## What this means

- **Without a battery:** for safety, the solar inverter switches off while the grid is down. It starts again by itself a few minutes after the power is back.
- **With a battery and backup:** the house may keep running on the battery. Use as little power as possible.

## What to do

1. Check whether the neighbours have power too. If not, it's a power cut: nothing to do but wait.
2. If only your house is affected: check the main switch, the residual-current device (RCD) and the fuses in the distribution board.
3. If a switch keeps tripping: leave it off and call an electrician or your installer.

! Don't work on the distribution board yourself unless you know how to do it safely.
