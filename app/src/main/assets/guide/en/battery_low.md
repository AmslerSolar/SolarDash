# Battery low

The battery is getting empty. When it's too low, the system switches off the house to protect the battery.

## What to do

1. **Use less power:** switch off large appliances such as heaters, kettles, washing machines and pumps.
2. If there's a generator: start it to charge the battery.
3. Check the solar panels: snow, leaves or heavy dirt reduce the charge a lot.
4. Charge comes back by itself with the sun. Avoid running the battery empty again and again: it shortens its life.

If the battery is often low although the sun shines, call your installer.
