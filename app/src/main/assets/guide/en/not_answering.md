# No connection

The tablet is on the right network, but the device doesn't answer.

## Is it night?

Many solar inverters without a battery **switch off completely at night**, including their communication. That's normal. They answer again in the morning when the sun is back.

## What to do

1. Check that the device has power: its lights or fan should be on. After a power cut, wait **5 minutes**.
2. Check the Wi-Fi or network box between the tablet and the device (for example a small RS485 or RS232 Wi-Fi adapter): its lights should be on. Unplug it for 10 seconds and plug it in again.
3. Close other apps or monitoring devices that read the same inverter. Many inverters only allow **one connection at a time**.
4. Wait 2 minutes: the app keeps trying by itself.

! Don't open the inverter or its connection boxes. They carry dangerous voltages, also from the solar panels and the battery when everything is switched off.

## Still not working?

Call your installer and tell them which device is shown.
